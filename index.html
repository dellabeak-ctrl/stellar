Method

The Lane–Emden equation was solved for n = 0 to 4.5 in steps of 0.05 with SciPy’s solve_ivp (DOP853, tolerances 10−12), starting from the central series θ ≈ 1 − ξ²/6 + nξ⁴/120. The solutions agree with a hand-written fourth-order Runge–Kutta solver to about 10−8 in ξ₁. Profiles are rescaled with ρ = ρcθn, P = Pcθn+1, T = Tcθ and m = qM, with ρc = Dnρ̄, Pc = WnGM²/R⁴ and the ideal-gas law for Tc. Polytropes are idealized models: the Sun is not a polytrope, so compare these profiles with a full solar model rather than treating them as predictions.
